# Style guide: voice, terms and form (terveystieto)

**Written for brief 002, 2026-11-21.** Every topic writer follows this guide and copies the form of the sample topic
`content/topics/01-terveys-voimavarana.md`. The contract (`docs/content-contract.md`) says *which* sections exist;
this guide says *how* they are written. Where they disagree, the contract wins. Base: the opinto-ohjaus style guide.

## 1. Voice

- **To the student (Opiskelijalle, Tehtävät, Pohdinta, Koetehtävät, Itseopiskelun askel, Apua ja tukea): `sinä`.**
  Warm, calm, factual. A capable 16-year-old reads alone at home; health is a resource to understand, not a test.
  - Yes: *Terveys ei ole joko–tai. Siihen vaikuttavat monet asiat, joista osa on omissa käsissä ja osa ei.*
  - No: *Huolehdi terveydestäsi, ettei sinulle käy huonosti!* / *Opiskelijan tulee…* (no threat, no third person).
- **To the teacher (Tuntisuunnitelmat, Eriyttäminen): collegial, short, practical.** Imperative, no pedagogy lecture.
  - Yes: *Piirrä kehät taululle ja kysy: mihin kehään koulumatka kuuluu?* / *Jos aika loppuu, tee K1 kotona.*
  - No: *Opettajan on tärkeää muistaa, että terveyskasvatus on moniulotteista.*
- **Explain, don't preach.** Describe mechanisms and evidence; never tell the student how to live.
  - Yes: *Uni vaikuttaa muistiin ja tunteiden säätelyyn.* No: *Sinun pitää nukkua enemmän.*
- **Population, not person.** Health facts are about people in general or fictional cases: *nuorilla*, *ihmisellä*,
  *Ellalla (kuvitteellinen)*, never *sinulla on luultavasti…*.

## 2. Plain Finnish (selkeä yleiskieli)

- One idea per sentence; aim under 15 words, break anything over 25. Paragraphs of 2–4 sentences.
- Verbs, not nouns: *uni palauttaa*, not *palautumisen tapahtuminen unen aikana*.
- Explain a term in the same or next sentence: *Voimavara tarkoittaa asiaa, joka auttaa jaksamaan ja toimimaan.*
- Small stable counts as words (*neljä tasoa, kolme osaa*). Never type a changing figure (§12).
- Headings below H2 are short nouns or questions: `### Mistä terveys syntyy?`
- No English loans when a Finnish word exists (*voimavara*, not *resurssi*; *hyvinvointi*, not *wellness*).
- Selkeä yleiskieli is not selkokieli; S2 and selkokieli support go in Eriyttäminen as a mini glossary.

## 4. "Osaan" outcomes

- `## Opiskelijalle` opens with `**Tämän aiheen jälkeen**` and 3–5 outcomes, each starting with **Osaan**.
- At least one outcome per cited code, mirroring its meaning in plain words. Never copy curriculum wording; the build
  prints the exact text in Lähteet. Outcomes describe knowledge and skills, never the student's own habits or values
  (assessment must not target them).
- Each outcome is practised: every task carries a line `**Harjoittelet:** Osaan 1 ja 3` and every exam task a line
  `*Harjoittelee: Osaan 2, 4*`. A checker can then confirm coverage by number.
- `## Pohdinta` ends with a self-assessment repeating the outcomes word for word on *En vielä · Osittain · Melko
  hyvin · Hyvin*.

## 5. Tasks (`## Tehtävät`)

```
### Tehtävä 1: Rakenna taustatekijöiden kartta
**Aika:** 20 min
**Tarvitset:** paperia ja kynän
**Ilman laitetta:** (how to do exactly the same alone, with paper and pen only; no printout, no partner needed)
**Harjoittelet:** Osaan 1 ja 3
1. … (at most 5 steps, one action each, imperative, sinä)
```

- **Times vary with the work** (e.g. 20, 10, 15). Never give every task the same time.
- At least one task uses an official page by a stable link; the offline line then says what to do on paper instead.
- Cases use fictional people marked *kuvitteellinen*, with mixed, neutral names (*Ella, Aaro, Sami, Noor, Kim, Veera*).
  The student analyses the case, never themselves. Steps end in a note the student keeps.

## 6. Pohdinta

- 2–3 prompts about ideas, communities or fictional cases, opened with *Kirjoita omaan muistioosi. Voit ohittaa
  kysymyksen.* Yes: *Mikä luokassa tai harrastusporukassa voi tukea jäsenten hyvinvointia?* No: *Miten sinä nukut?*

## 7. Exam tasks (`## Koetehtävät`)

- The exam has three parts: **A20** (choose 1 of 3, content only), **B20** (2 of 3), **C30** (2 of 3); 120 points.
  A topic with three tasks uses one of each part; with two, one B20 and one A20 or C30. The section opens with one
  line: TE1 alone covers only part of the exam; TE2 and TE3 carry the rest.
- Heading `### K1: Virheiden tunnistaminen, 25 min (20 p)` (engine form), then `*Osa A20 · Harjoittelee: Osaan 1,
  5*`, then the task. Sub-parts `a) … (8 p)` add up to the heading. Practice minutes: A20 20–30, B20 30–40, C30 45–60.
- Type slugs (front matter `type`). Existing: `oikein-vaarin`, `virheiden-tunnistaminen` (five errors, 4 p each),
  `vertailu`, `taulukon-taydentaminen`, `teorian-soveltaminen`, `aineiston-analyysi`, `eettinen-analyysi`,
  `tutkimussuunnitelma`. New (templates in §7a): `luettele-ja-luokittele`, `maarittele-ja-esimerkki`, `monivalinta`,
  `kasitteiden-nimeaminen`, `piirtaminen`, `aineiston-lukeminen`, `viitekehyksen-soveltaminen`,
  `kasitteen-kritiikki`. Vary types across a module; no two topics share the same C30 sub-part split.
- Material is self-written and labelled `*Aineisto (kuvitteellinen)*`, or a linked open or official source. Never
  text from publishers or YTL, never a past exam task reworded: mirror the format, write our own content.
- **Self-made data** (chart, table, map, image) is a Markdown table or a described picture, never an image file:
  `*Taulukko 1 (kuvitteellinen): otsikko*` or `*Kuvaus kuvasta (kuvitteellinen): …*`. Figures are round, about
  groups, opinions, environments or services, and never about bodies, food or nutrient amounts, calories, doses,
  substance use or hours of sleep or exercise. Flagged pages use material without figures (categories, order,
  described labels). A hypnogram is a table of sleep stages by sleep cycle (*jakso 1–5*), with no clock hours.
- Tasks ask about concepts, mechanisms, cases and society; never the student's own health or behaviour.

## 7a. Templates for the new formats

Each template gives the task line, the scoring, and the model-answer pattern in two halves: *sisältö* (what earns
content points) and *tiedonkäsittely* (the rubric half; in A20 written as feedback without points). Points are
examples; keep the sum equal to the heading. A count or a penalty never takes a task below 0 p.

**Luettele ja luokittele** (`luettele-ja-luokittele`, A20 or B20). *Tehtävä:* "Luettele kahdeksan [asiaa] ja
luokittele ne [luokat]. Perustele luokka lyhyesti." Name the number and the classes in the task.
- *Pisteytys (count rubric):* each distinct item in the right class with a reason 2 p (item 1 p, class and reason
  1 p), counted up to the number asked; repeats and extra items 0 p. A20: 8 × 2 p + 4 p for one link between
  classes; B20 content half: 5 × 2 p.
- *Malli, sisältö:* a table `| Asia | Luokka | Perustelu |` with exactly the number asked.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* a paragraph that compares the classes and judges which matter most for the case.

**Määrittele ja anna esimerkki** (`maarittele-ja-esimerkki`, A20). *Tehtävä:* "Määrittele käsitteet A–D ja anna
kustakin terveyteen liittyvä esimerkki." An optional character limit trains concision.
- *Pisteytys:* per concept 5 p: core definition 2 p, a fitting example 2 p, the difference from a near concept 1 p.
- *Malli, sisältö:* per concept one definition sentence, one example sentence (a fictional case), one contrast.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* feedback on precision: the definition names the essential features, not a synonym.

**Monivalinta, väärästä vähennys** (`monivalinta`, A20). *Tehtävä:* ten items, options a–d, one right. The
instruction says: "Oikea vastaus 2 p, väärä −1 p, tyhjä 0 p. Tehtävän pisteet eivät mene alle nollan."
- Variant with justification: four items × 5 p (right choice 1 p, a reason that uses a concept 4 p), no penalty.
- *Malli, sisältö:* the right letter and one line why it is right and why the most tempting wrong option is wrong.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* feedback on which items test a distinction (for example prevention versus promotion).

**Käsitteiden nimeäminen** (`kasitteiden-nimeaminen`, A20 or a B20 sub-part). *Tehtävä:* short descriptions or
fictional cases (*Kim (kuvitteellinen) …*); the student names the concept each one shows, with a one-line reason.
- *Pisteytys:* per item 2 p: the concept 1 p (glossary synonyms accepted, §3), the reason tying case and concept 1 p.
- *Malli, sisältö:* a numbered list `1. medikalisaatio: arkinen asia selitetään sairautena, koska …`.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* feedback on telling near concepts apart (suojaava tekijä versus voimavara).

**Piirrä malli tai kuvio** (`piirtaminen`, a sub-part of B20 or C30). *Tehtävä:* "Piirrä vastausarkille [malli]
ja selitä …" or "Laadi taulukon 1 tiedoista pylväskuvio." The student draws on paper or in an editor.
- *Pisteytys:* the drawing 3–5 p by element (boxes or stages in order, arrows, the return path; for a chart: title,
  both axes named with units, a scale from zero, a legend, the source marked *kuvitteellinen*); the explanation
  carries the rest.
- *Malli, sisältö:* the drawing as a text figure in a code block (`esiharkinta → harkinta → …`) or a list of the
  chart's elements, then the explanation stage by stage.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* what the model or chart shows and what it hides (a model simplifies; a chart of
  fictional groups shows no cause).

**Lue kuvio, taulukko, kartta tai kuva** (`aineiston-lukeminen`, B20 or C30). *Tehtävä:* self-made data (§7) and
three steps: "a) Kuvaile, mitä aineistossa näkyy. b) Selitä ilmiötä terveystiedon käsittein. c) Arvioi aineiston
rajoja." The 5 p deduction for unused material applies.
- *Pisteytys:* readings cited from the data (rows, groups, changes) 1 p each; explanations with a mechanism; the
  limits (fictional, a single measurement, correlation is not cause).
- *Malli, sisältö:* sentences that name the row or group: *Taulukon 1 mukaan ryhmässä B …*, then the mechanism.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* compares groups or stages, judges what the data can and cannot show, concludes.

**Sovella annettua viitekehystä** (`viitekehyksen-soveltaminen`, B20 or C30). *Tehtävä:* the framework stated in
our own words with a dated link (for example the five action areas of health promotion, or the four levels of
determinants), plus a fictional case: "Tee kustakin osa-alueesta yksi perusteltu ehdotus."
- *Pisteytys:* per element: a concrete measure 1 p, who acts 1 p, the mechanism to health 1 p (C30: 5 × 3 p = 15 p).
- *Malli, sisältö:* one paragraph per element, its name in bold, tied to a detail of the case.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* judges feasibility, cost and who is reached, and which measure works best and why.

**Arvioi käsitettä kriittisesti** (`kasitteen-kritiikki`, B20 or C30). *Tehtävä:* a concept or definition
paraphrased with a dated link (never quoted at length): "Arvioi määritelmän vahvuuksia ja heikkouksia."
- *Pisteytys:* strengths and weaknesses named and explained, 2–3 p each (for a definition of health: many-sided
  versus static, an ideal no one reaches, a risk of medicalisation, hard to measure), an alternative view 2–3 p.
- *Malli, sisältö:* a strength paragraph, a weakness paragraph, an alternative with its source.
- *Malli, tiedonkäsittely:* weighs both sides, places the concept in its time and use, ends in a reasoned verdict.

## 8. Model answers (`## Mallivastaukset`)

For each task an H3 that repeats the task heading exactly (`### K1: Virheiden tunnistaminen, 25 min (20 p)`). Then:
1. **Malliksi kelpaava vastaus**: original wording, full sentences, the length a good kokelas writes; in B20 and
   C30 both the content and the information-processing work are visible (§7a patterns).
2. **Pisteytys**:
   - *Sisältö*: points per required element, as a list that adds up (`- … 4 p`).
   - *Tiedonkäsittely* (B20 and C30): half of the points (B20 10 + 10, C30 15 + 15), judged as a whole on the five
     criteria *käsitteiden käyttö, soveltaminen, analysointi, arviointi, argumentointi*, in three bands (B20: 0–3 /
     4–7 / 8–10; C30: 0–5 / 6–11 / 12–15), each with one line on what it means in this task. Its first line links
     the shared rubric, `[tiedonkäsittelyn arviointi](../../opetussuunnitelma/)`: the section *Tiedonkäsittely
     koetehtävissä* of the curriculum page (engine-issues E-11).
   - A20: content only, all points; the five criteria are given as feedback without points.
   - Every task with material ends its scoring with: `- Jos vastaus ei viittaa aineistoon, kokonaispisteistä
     vähennetään 5 p.`
   - A last line `**Yhteensä:** 20 p` that equals the heading.
3. **Tyypilliset virheet**: 3–5 bullets, e.g. *luettelee tekijöitä selittämättä mekanismia*.
4. The fixed line, verbatim: `Pisteytys on sivuston oma harjoitusohje, ei ylioppilastutkintolautakunnan.`

## 9. Self-study and lesson plans

- `## Itseopiskelun askel`: **Tavoite**, **Aikaa kuluu**, **Näin etenet** (numbered: lue → tee → harjoittele
  koetehtävä → pohdi → seuraava askel), **Muistiinpanoihin** (what to keep for exam revision).
- One H2 plan `## Tuntisuunnitelma (75 min)` with H3 `(60 min)` and `(45 min)` inside it (E-6). It opens
  with `**Tavoitetehtävä:**` naming the goal task.
  Columns: `| Vaihe | Min | Toiminta | Opettajan rooli | Vaihtoehto | Jos aika loppuu |`.
  `Min` a bare integer, no total row. 75 min template: Virittäytyminen 5 / Aiemman tiedon aktivointi 10 / Lyhyt
  alustus 10 / Työskentely 25 / Pari- tai pienryhmäpohdinta 10 / Koeharjoitus ja exit 10 / Puskuri 5.
- **The 45-minute plan keeps the goal task whole**; cut the warm-up, the discussion and the exam practice first.
- After the 45-minute table: `### Valmistelu` (≤ 5 lines), `### Avoimet kysymykset` (5–8), `### Pelisäännöt ja
  oletusten tarkistus`.
- `## Eriyttäminen`: **Helpompi polku**, **Syvempi polku**, **S2 ja selkeä kieli** (mini glossary), **Lukeminen,
  keskittyminen ja jännitys**, **Ilman laitetta**, **Tuki**.

## 10. Safety and inclusion: do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| *Jos sinulla on oireita tai huolia, ota yhteyttä opiskeluterveydenhuoltoon.* | *Jos väsyttää, kokeile magnesiumia.* (advice) |
| *Ruokavalio voi tukea jaksamista monella tavalla.* | *Syö alle X kcal / ihannepaino on…* (figures) |
| *Kehot ovat erilaisia; terveys ei näy painosta.* | *Ylipaino on vaarallista.* / before–after examples |
| *Kumppani, puoliso, ystävä* ; *kaikki sukupuolet* | *poikaystävä ja tyttöystävä* as the only pair |
| *Ella (kuvitteellinen) asuu kaukana harrastuksista.* | *Kerro ryhmälle omista elintavoistasi.* |
| *Monet tekijät eivät ole yksilön valittavissa.* | *Huono terveys on omaa syytä.* (shaming) |
| *Tupakointi lisää sairauksien riskiä* (with a link). | Shock images, death counts, *sinä voit kuolla* |
| *kuoli itsemurhaan*; help links next to the text | Methods, places, "epidemic" language, reasons as simple causes |

- A page with `safety_flags` opens with the `content_note`, one plain sentence: *Tällä sivulla käsitellään
  syömistä ja painoa. Sivulla ei ole lukuja eikä vertailuja, ja lopussa on tietoa avusta.*
- Figures are banned on flagged pages (kg, kcal, BMI, grams, portions, doses, hours of sleep or minutes of exercise).
- Never assume a home, two parents, money, a body type, a gender, an orientation, a religion or Finnish as the first
  language. Examples rotate names, families and settings.

## 11. Help box (`## Apua ja tukea`)

Recommended on every page, required on flagged pages. Three parts, in order:
1. **Bold first line** for emergencies: *Jos olet välittömässä vaarassa, soita hätänumeroon 112.*
2. One or two sentences: for own symptoms or worries go to opiskeluterveydenhuolto (terveydenhoitaja, lääkäri),
   kuraattori or psykologi; asking for help is normal.
3. **Apua saat myös:** a list of dated links with a short gloss: *[MIELI Kriisipuhelin (luettu 21.11.2026)](url):
   keskusteluapua kriisissä, myös nimettömästi.* Phone numbers other than 112 are linked, not typed.

## 12. Facts, links and materials

- Stable rules may be stated in original words. Changing values (recommendations' figures, statistics, ages in
  pending laws, phone numbers, service hours) are linked, never typed. Link a stable parent page.
- Every body link is in `official_links` with `read: YYYY-MM-DD` from a page you opened; list facts in `yearly_facts`.
- Existing materials come from `data/materials.yaml` with their `licence_class`. Link-out and CC BY-SA content is
  never inlined or paraphrased closely; CC BY is linked, adapted only with credit, and in TE1 normally just linked.
- A page you cannot open after two tries is left out or replaced by an opened parent, and reported.

## 13. Files and length

- `content/topics/NN-slug.md`; `id: te1-NN-slug` (the engine adds the module). Slugs: 01 terveys-voimavarana, 02 elintapamallit,
  03 terveyskulttuuri, 04 ravinto, 05 liikunta-ja-uni, 06 seksuaaliterveys, 07 seksuaalioikeudet, 08 mielenterveys,
  09 stressi-ja-kriisit, 10 opiskeluhyvinvointi.
- Whole topic 3,000–5,500 words, under 500 lines; Opiskelijalle 300–600 words.
- `## Lähteet` holds one line: `Kootaan sivun tiedoista automaattisesti.` No hand-written Linkitetyt materiaalit (E-3).

## 14. Never

- Never type curriculum wording, or present something as required by the curriculum when it is not.
- Never give medical advice, diagnose, or suggest a treatment, supplement or diet.
- Never ask the student to disclose health, body, sexuality, substance use or family matters, to the site or class.
- Never use fear, shock, shame or moralising; never link health to worth or to a body size.
- Never copy text, pictures or tasks from publishers, YTL, OPH or aoe.fi; never reword a past YTL task.
- Never name real private people; never write a local path, a key or a personal e-mail.
- Never embed images, scripts, fonts or iframes from other hosts; plain links only.

## 15. TE2 *Terveys ja ympäristö*: additions (brief 004, 2026-11-21)

**Material-based tasks.** TE2 tasks are mostly material-based: in a topic of three, at least two carry material; one
concept task without material (A20, `maarittele-ja-esimerkki` or `kasitteiden-nimeaminen`) is kept across the module.
New type slugs beside §7a:
- `tutkimustiedon-arviointi` (B20 or C30): two fictional studies in one table (who, purpose, asetelma, otos with n and
  response rate, mittari, result, where published). Shares go in words (*yhdeksän kymmenestä*): the build rejects
  `%`. a) compare on named aspects, b) explain a result with a concept, c) judge what a claim may conclude. *Pisteytys:* each aspect compared both ways 2 p; the concept named 1 p and
  explained 1–2 p; the verdict tied to the table.
- `viestinnan-analyysi` (B20 or C30): a fictional advert, post, news snippet or chatbot answer to a non-medical
  question. a) name the vaikutuskeinot or vinoumat with the line they appear in, b) explain how each misleads,
  c) rewrite or answer the asker. *Pisteytys:* per item 2 p (name 1 p, quoted line and mechanism 1 p).
- `kuvion-tulkinta` (B20 or C30): a fictional table turned into a described chart, or the reverse; as `piirtaminen`
  plus `aineiston-lukeminen`.

Content ladder (B20 content half): 2 / 4 / 6 aspects explained = 3 / 6 / 9 p, +1 p for a link between them. C30
content half: 1–4 perspectives = 4 / 7 / 10 / 13 p, +2 p for a weighed conclusion. Referring to at least one
material is enough to avoid the 5 p deduction (§8). Every table is a Markdown table with its data in the task, so the
offline line can read *Tämä on paperitehtävä: aineisto on tehtävässä.* Material forms by topic: results tables
(01, 02, 04, 08); two data sources compared (02); advert, post or chatbot answer (03); a school indoor-air or noise
table or a council news item with a framework (04–06); a school safety plan summary or a non-graphic fictional case
(07); a workplace survey and memo (08); a qualitative leaflet, campaign or interview excerpt with no amounts (09, 10).

**TE2 safety rules** (pages flagged `violence` or `substances`: 07, 09, 10; §10–11 apply in full):
- `content_note`: what the page covers, that the student may step out, and that nobody has to share anything.
- No invented statistics on flagged pages: qualitative fictional material, or linked, dated official figures. A
  social-norms line (*useimmat nuoret eivät …*) only with a linked, dated survey, else left out.
- No amounts, doses, routes, prices, mixing, ingredients or how-to; no stakes, odds or losses; no methods,
  perpetrator tactics or graphic detail beyond recognising coercion. No testimonies, shock stories or guest speakers
  who tell their worst case.
- Dependence is a health condition with biological, psychological and social causes; never weakness or a choice of
  character. Gaming is not a dependence by default. Design features (vaihteleva palkkio, läheltä piti, loot box) are
  taught as media literacy. No scoring self-test.
- Violence: the responsibility is always with the person who uses it. Every gender, same-sex relationships, every
  family form; digital violence included. Use the law's wording with its dated link; sexual violence links te1-07.
- Cases are fictional third-person, end in a non-violent step and a route to help, and never ask about the student's
  own or family's use, gambling or experience of violence.
- The lesson plan's *Pelisäännöt* says: the school nurse and student welfare team are available; a disclosure leads
  to an adult's duty to act, and the child-welfare report for under-18s is explained neutrally; a series of lessons
  works better than a single one.
- Help box from the audience-verified youth list only (112, opiskeluterveydenhuolto, Sekasin and Apuu, Nuortenlinkki
  with its hours, Nettiturvakoti under 18, Nettiturvis, Väestöliitto chat under 29). Nollalinja, RIKU and Peluuri only
  as *myös*-links with their stated audience, never called youth services. Each link carries its read date.
- Debate tasks (substance policy, gambling markets) keep both sides; the model answer says prevention is curriculum
  content, not optional, and states the legal status as it is today, with a dated link.
