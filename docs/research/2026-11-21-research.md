## In plain words

- **Terveystieto has three 2-credit modules:**
  - TE1 *Terveys voimavarana* (compulsory, id 6834847);
  - TE2 *Terveys ja ympäristö* (national optional, id 6834848);
  - TE3 *Terveys ja yhteiskunta* (national optional, id 6834849).

  All are graded 4–10 [A, spot-checked in the ePerusteet API].
- **TE1 has 3 objectives and 7 content areas** in two groups, the whole of health and the basics of physical and
  mental wellbeing [A]:
  - the determinants of health;
  - four named social-psychology models (the theory of planned behaviour, social-cognitive theory, the health belief
    model, the transtheoretical model);
  - health-culture phenomena (medicalisation, alternative treatments);
  - nutrition, exercise, rest, sleep and weight management;
  - sexuality, sexual health and sexual rights;
  - protective factors of mental health, stress and crises;
  - study wellbeing.
- **My expected answer was wrong on content:** substances and dependence, and source criticism and health
  communication, belong to TE2, not TE1 [A].
- **Assessment must not target the student's values, attitudes or health behaviour** [A, spot-checked]. That fits the
  site's privacy rule: no task asks the student to disclose their own health.
- **The matriculation exam:** 120 points, 9 tasks in three parts (choose 1 of three 20-point tasks, 2 of three
  20-point tasks, 2 of three 30-point tasks), many of them material-based [A, from YTL's scoring documents]. Past
  tasks are not openly licensed, so the site links them and writes only original tasks [A].
- **Existing materials:** almost everything is link-out (THL, Ruokavirasto, UKK, Mielenterveystalo, MIELI, Nyyti,
  Väestöliitto, Sexpo, Seta, Duodecim, OPH's support material). A few items on aoe.fi are open [A, spot-checked]:
  - 1113, a TE1 course shell from Schildtin lukio (CC BY-SA, so linked, not adapted);
  - 1852, a TE01 plan (CC BY);
  - 4704, reaali exam-style tasks (CC BY; check first that none are taken from YTL);
  - 3215, a breast and menstrual health package (CC BY, lower-secondary level).
- **New in 2026:** from autumn 2026 every second-degree student does a digital health survey in student health care
  [A, OPH news 28.4.2026]. Nicotine pouches are banned for minors to possess; the alcohol act has a pending
  amendment [M, likely].
- **Help lines:** Kriisipuhelin 09 2525 0111 is open 24/7 in Finnish and Swedish (MIELI) [A, spot-checked]; 112 for
  emergencies.

## Not verified, and what that means for the site

- **Sleep:** no official Finnish recommendation was found, and secondary sources conflict. The site states no hours.
- **Physical activity:** UKK's figures were read only in summaries. Link the UKK recommendation page; state no
  minutes.
- **Law texts:** Terveydenhuoltolaki § 17 (only an old version was read), the drug law, and the alcohol and tobacco
  acts' current text. TE1 states no legal rules beyond age limits, and links them.
- **Finnish safe-messaging sources:** the MIELI toolkit, the journalists' council's recommendation and eating-disorder
  writing guidance were not read. The WHO 2023 guidance and UNESCO 2018 were [A]. The site's rules marked [M] must
  be confirmed by the safe-messaging reviewer.
