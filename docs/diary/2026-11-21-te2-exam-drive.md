# Brief 009: TE2 exam drive, coverage target met

**Date:** 2026-11-21 **IDs:** brief 009

## What happened

Three fixers added the cross-check's fact gaps to the ten TE2 pages: research vocabulary and risk concepts, radon,
indoor air and water, loneliness and FOMO, climate anxiety, working-life change, and substance harms and alcohol policy
in words. New formats: a 20-item true/false set, list-and-count tasks, a described risk map, a two-series chart, a
described video, and stated character limits. The re-mapper put full coverage of TE2-touching exam points at 74.4%
(strict reading 71.2%), up from 57.7%, with none at 0%. The judge rejected the first round over six small items: two
missing citations, one citation that did not back its claim (alcohol warning labels, now removed), a wrong fraction and
two scoring lists that gave a sub-part no content points. The producer fixed them, and a separate recheck ruled on the
fixes.

## Traps

- **Citation drift.** A fix round added "warning labels" under an STM link whose own note said the page doesn't
  mention them. The yearly-facts note caught it, so keep writing those notes.
- **The line cap bites.** Four pages sit at 499–500 lines. Fixers compressed lesson-plan rows and model answers to fit,
  which is fine once, but TE2 cannot grow further without splitting topics.

## Heuristic

When a C30 task has sub-parts, the content-point list must name every sub-part; check sums against the task's own
points, not only against 15.

**Seed:** Should the engine check that each exam sub-part's points appear in the content scoring (a new E-12)?
