# Brief 013: figure mentions, small fixes, no review banner

**Date:** 2026-11-22 **IDs:** brief 013, D-008

## What happened

The operator removed the review banner (D-008). The site template override existed only for it, so it was deleted and
the engine's template applies again. Three fixers named every one of the 22 diagrams and charts in its page's text with
a short "kaavio tämän osion lopussa" phrase, trading weaker words where a section sat at the 600-word limit. The
life-expectancy chart now shows its source, licence and read date and labels 2025; the transition chart has short
phase labels; the type 2 diabetes sentence follows its source again. Four illustrations were redone; the judge
rejected two of them and one misplaced mention, which the producer fixed and a recheck ruled on.

## Traps

- **The engine places a figure at the end of its whole section,** so a figure meant for one model answer lands after
  the last one. The mention says where it is; finer placement is an engine gap.
- **A mention can sit on the wrong sentence.** The ethics diagram illustrates the wallet table, not the sentence about
  conflicting viewpoints.

## Heuristic

Put a figure's mention on the sentence whose content the figure draws, not on the nearest related sentence.

**Seed:** Should the engine accept a subsection as a picture's placement, so figures can sit beside the model answer
they illustrate (E-13)?
