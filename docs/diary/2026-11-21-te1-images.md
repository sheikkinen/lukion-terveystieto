# Brief 007: TE1 images, accepted with minors

**Date:** 2026-11-21 **IDs:** brief 007, D-005, D-006

## What happened

Six Mermaid diagrams and a Vega-Lite hypnogram were rendered to real-text SVG ahead of the build (no Mermaid script in
the browser), and twelve p-image illustrations were made under the health rules, for $0.085. The judge rejected once:
the hypnogram was unreadable on a phone, one diagram's labels did not match its wrapped lines, one illustration broke
the style, and the render script held local Chrome paths. One redo round fixed all four.

## Traps

- **Mermaid's SVGs are bigger than measured in research** (37–61 KB, not 18 KB), because stadium shapes carry
  long-precision paths; the render script now rounds numbers.
- **A chart drawn for desktop is unreadable on a phone.** Size charts for 343 px first.

## Heuristic

Design every chart and diagram at phone width first, and check its text size there.

**Seed:** Should the engine render `content/diagrams/*.mmd` itself, so re-rendering never depends on a local Chrome?
