# Brief 005: the TE1 site on the shared engine

**Date:** 2026-11-21 **IDs:** brief 005

## What happened

A builder wrote `subject.yaml`, the six pages, the Pages workflow and a template override for the review banner,
and fixed the content to the engine's formats (file names, exam headings, lesson-plan headings, a `linked_materials`
key). Three checkers passed the rendered site; the judge rejected on line limits, the missing materials list and the
contract. A fix round listed the linked materials in *Lähteet*; the producer wrote the contract addendum and unlinked
an OPH page written for schools from the help boxes.

## Traps

- **An engine shapes the content.** Ten gaps between the contract and the engine surfaced only at the first build;
  they are in `docs/engine-issues.md`.
