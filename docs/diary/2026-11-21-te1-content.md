# Brief 002: TE1 content, accepted with minors after two fix rounds

**Date:** 2026-11-21 **IDs:** brief 002

## What happened

A data agent, a lead writer (topic 01 and the style guide) and three writers produced the ten TE1 topics with exam
tasks and model answers. Four checkers and the judge rejected the first version: topic 06 linked Sexpo's SeriE as
anonymous help for students, though SeriE serves people whose sexual interest is directed at children; topic 07's
model answer treated diversity as optional; the link inventory was incomplete. Two fix rounds closed them. The
producer finally unlinked a THL page written for professionals from the help boxes.

## Traps

- **A link that opens (200) is not a link that fits.** SeriE's page opened fine; nobody read what it is for until
  the facts checker did. Every help link must be read for its audience.
- **"Both sides" in a model answer can make curriculum content look optional.**

## Heuristic

For every help link, record who the page is for, read on the page, before it goes in a help box.

**Seed:** Should the build refuse a help-box link whose materials entry has an audience other than students?
