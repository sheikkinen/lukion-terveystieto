## Expected answers, written before the work [M]

1. **Curriculum.** Terveystieto in LOPS 2019 has one compulsory module, TE1 *Terveys voimavarana* (2 op), and two
   national optional modules, TE2 and TE3. It is graded 4–10. TE1 covers health as a whole and as a resource: the
   factors behind health; sleep, exercise, nutrition; mental health and study wellbeing; sexual health and
   relationships; substances and dependence; health literacy and sources. TE2 is about health, the environment and
   society, and TE3 about health research and health in the world (names to be read from ePerusteet).
2. **Existing materials to link.** Official and NGO material is plentiful but not lesson-shaped:
   - THL (Kouluterveyskysely, recommendations);
   - Ruokavirasto (nutrition recommendations);
   - UKK-instituutti (physical-activity recommendations);
   - Mielenterveystalo, MIELI ry and Nyyti (mental health);
   - Väestöliitto and Sexpo (sexual health);
   - EHYT and A-klinikka (substances);
   - OPH's TE support material;
   - the CC BY-SA TE1 course shell on aoe.fi (Schildtin lukio).

   Most are link-out (all rights reserved); a few may be CC BY or CC BY-SA.
3. **Facts that change:** nutrition and activity recommendations, laws (alcohol, tobacco and nicotine, drugs), the
   services after the 2023 reform to wellbeing services counties, student health care, crisis lines. They are linked,
   with dates.
4. **Exam tasks.** The terveystieto matriculation exam uses material-based tasks, short concept tasks and essays.
   YTL publishes past exams and "hyvän vastauksen piirteet", which may be linked but not copied. Original
   exam-style tasks with model answers and a scoring guide suit the site.
5. **Safety.** Suicide, self-harm and eating disorders need safe-messaging guidelines (Finnish and international),
   and sexuality education should follow evidence-based guidance (e.g. UNESCO's technical guidance and the THL or
   Väestöliitto materials). Scare tactics do not work in substance education.
6. **TE1 plan.** About 8–9 topics, each a lesson and a self-study step, with a 75/60/45-minute plan, tasks, a
   reflection, exam-style tasks with model answers, and a list of linked materials with their licence class.

## The work

`scout.js` with six researchers: the curriculum (TE1–TE3 from the ePerusteet API, assessment, the law that touches
health education); existing materials to link (per TE1 content area: publisher, URL, audience, licence class,
quality); facts and services that change; the terveystieto exam and exam-task design (link-only use of YTL
material); safety and inclusion guidelines (safe messaging, sexuality education, substance education, privacy for
minors); and the opinto-ohjaus site's contract, engine and lessons as the base (read-only, local). Then the critic
proposes the TE1 topic list with codes, an outline for TE2 and TE3, the contract additions for a graded subject,
the link inventory per topic, and the expert review plan.
