# 004: TE2 *Terveys ja ympäristö*: research, then ten topics with exam tasks and links

**Written before the work.** Producer's brief, 2026-11-21. Two workflows: `scout.js` for research, then
`module-content.js` (a module-general copy of `te1-content.js`) for the content.

## Why

The operator [O]: "after public, continue to next terveystieto course". TE2 is the next module after TE1 (G-2).

**Serves:** G-2. **Goal type:** research, then technical (checks) with look. **Chain:** brief 4. **Models:** as
briefs 001 and 002: researchers and writers on Sonnet, the lead writer, critic and judge on Opus.

What stands [A]:
- **TE2's curriculum** (`data/curriculum.yaml`): 5 objectives (TE2.t1–t5) and 8 content areas in three groups:
  - information: research and everyday knowledge, biases, the research process; health communication (s1–s2);
  - environment and safety: the built, natural and psychosocial environment; sustainable development; safety and
    violence prevention; wellbeing at work (s3–s6);
  - pleasure and dependence: the mechanisms of dependence; substance and behavioural dependences, their causes,
    harms and prevention (s7–s8).
- **TE1's contract, style guide, safety rules and process apply.** The TE1 lessons apply: every help link read for its
  audience, offline tasks on paper only, "both sides" never making curriculum content optional.
- The exam cross-check (brief 003) says what TE2 must carry; its findings feed the topic list when they are in.

## Expected answer, written before the work [M]

1. About ten topics:
   - scientific and everyday knowledge, and biases;
   - the research process and judging research;
   - health communication and its means;
   - the built and natural environment and health;
   - the psychosocial environment;
   - sustainable development and health;
   - safety and violence prevention;
   - wellbeing at work;
   - how dependence forms;
   - substance and behavioural dependences: causes, harms, prevention.
2. **Links:**
   - research and sources: THL and Duodecim;
   - environment: Ilmatieteen laitos (air quality), Työterveyslaitos, the Ympäristöministeriö;
   - violence: Nollalinja, Rikosuhripäivystys, Väestöliitto and MIELI;
   - wellbeing at work: Työterveyslaitos;
   - dependence: EHYT, A-klinikkasäätiö and Päihdelinkki, THL, Peluuri for gambling, the Sosiaali- ja
     terveysministeriö.

   Almost all link-out.
3. **Safety:**
   - violence and dependence pages need content notes and help boxes with student-facing services;
   - substance education must avoid scare tactics and give no amounts or methods;
   - the safety reviewer must cover violence and substances.
4. **TE2 suits material-based exam tasks** (research summaries, graphs, communication examples). Its exam tasks
   should be mostly material-based, with self-written material labelled *kuvitteellinen*.

## The work

1. **Research** (`scout.js`), six researchers:
   - the TE2 curriculum in depth;
   - materials to link per TE2 content area, each read for its audience and licence;
   - facts and laws that change (substance, gambling and violence-related laws and services; air quality;
     occupational health);
   - exam-task design for material-based tasks;
   - safety and inclusion for violence and dependence;
   - what TE2 must carry for the exam (from brief 003's results if ready, else from YTL's public documents).

   The critic proposes the topic list with codes, the link inventory and the safety flags.
2. **Content** (`module-content.js`): a data agent and the lead writer (TE2 topic 01, style-guide additions) in
   parallel; three writers; four checkers (curriculum, facts and links, safety and inclusion, pedagogy and exam
   tasks); one fix round; the judge on the final recheck.
3. The producer commits and pushes (the repository is public by then).

## Limits

€0. No images. Nothing copied from publishers, YTL or other sites. Help links only to services meant for young
people.

## Done when

The operator can read ten TE2 topics on GitHub, each with linked materials, exam-style tasks with model answers, and
safe handling of violence and dependence. Internally: every TE2 code covered by a topic and an exam task, the checks
passed or named, and the judge accepts.

## Addendum 2026-11-21: the engine formats and the TE1 lessons, before the content work

Written before step 2. The site now runs on `lukio-runko` (brief 005), so TE2 follows the contract addendum's
formats: files `content/topics/NN-slug.md` with `module: TE2`, ids `te2-NN-slug`, exam headings
`### K1: Nimi, 25 min (20 p)`, one H2 lesson plan with H3 60/45, `linked_materials`. TE2 is added to `subject.yaml`, and
its codes are fetched with `lukio-runko fetch-curriculum`. Model answers split into content and information-processing
halves and link the rubric section (brief 006). The exam formats from the style guide's §7a are used, with a varied
30-point split per topic. Material-based tasks use self-made tables or charts, but flagged pages (violence,
substances) use material without figures. The TE2 research (`docs/research/2026-11-21-te2-scouting.json`) gives the
topic list, the link inventory and the help-link audiences: only services whose own pages say they serve young people
go into help boxes. Images for TE2 are a separate later brief.
