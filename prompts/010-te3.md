# 010: TE3 *Terveys ja yhteiskunta*: research, then ten topics with exam tasks and links

**Written before the work.** Producer's brief, 2026-11-21. Two workflows: `scout.js` for research, then
`module-content.js` for the content, plus a re-mapper against the exam mapping before the judge.

## Why

The operator [O]: "Start TE3", in answer to "Go on to TE3 Terveys ja yhteiskunta?" after the TE2 exam drive
(brief 009). TE3 is the last national module (G-2).

**Serves:** G-2. **Goal type:** research, then technical (checks and an exam re-mapping). **Chain:** brief 10.
**Models:** researchers and writers on Sonnet; the lead writer, critic and judge on Opus; checkers and the re-mapper
on Sonnet.

What stands [A]:
- **TE3's curriculum** (`data/curriculum-te1-te3.yaml`, ePerusteet module 6834849): 4 objectives (TE3.t1–t4) and 10
  content areas in three groups:
  - health promotion and disease prevention over time: health concepts and explanatory models of disease; the
    social, scientific and technological factors behind population health; future health challenges (s1–s3);
  - disease and care: the epidemiological transition, the main non-communicable and infectious diseases; how social
    and health services are organised, produced and used; self-care and own care (s4–s6);
  - ethics: the viewpoints of ethical analysis (virtues, consequences, rights, duties, motives, values, norms); key
    ethical and legal questions (fertility treatment, abortion, freedom and responsibility in lifestyles, screening,
    gene technology, euthanasia); the ethical principles of health care, patients' rights, prioritisation; health
    and health inequality in Finland and globally (s7–s10).
- **What the exam needs from TE3** (brief 003, `docs/research/2026-11-21-exam-cross-check.json`; brief 008's report):
  TE3-only tasks are about 21% of exam points. TE3 must carry disease epidemiology (cancer risk factors, COPD,
  incidence and prevalence), infectious disease including waterborne and tick-borne, health inequality with figure
  reading, Health in All Policies as policy history, the ethical-viewpoints framework, and self-care and services.
  Brief 009's partial TE2 rows that belong here: water treatment and waterborne infection, measuring functional
  capacity, and bone health as a disease-prevention case.
- **The lessons of TE1 and TE2 apply:** every help link read for its audience; offline tasks on paper only; exam
  formats from the style guide's §7a, including the 20-item true/false set, list-and-count and material described in
  text; character limits where the exam states them; every C30 sub-part named in the content points; every new fact a
  dated link whose page states it; and the line cap, so each topic aims at 460 lines or fewer to leave room.

## Expected answer, written before the work [M]

1. **About ten topics:**
   - health concepts and explanatory models of disease, through history;
   - how society, science and technology changed population health (the epidemiological transition, the North
     Karelia project, Health in All Policies);
   - non-communicable diseases: cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, cancers, COPD, memory disorders, with risk
     factors and incidence and prevalence;
   - infectious diseases: transmission, vaccines and herd immunity, antibiotic resistance, waterborne and tick-borne
     disease, sexually transmitted infections;
   - social and health services: the wellbeing services counties, who to go to in which situation, public, private
     and third sector;
   - self-care and own care, and medicines;
   - the viewpoints of ethical analysis;
   - ethical and legal questions: fertility treatment, abortion, screening, gene technology, euthanasia;
   - health-care ethics, patients' rights and prioritisation;
   - health inequality in Finland and globally, and future health challenges.
2. **Links:** THL (disease registers, vaccination programme, health inequality), Terveyskirjasto, Käypä hoito, the
   Sosiaali- ja terveysministeriö, Finlex (patients' rights act, abortion act, fertility treatment act), ETENE (the
   national ethics board), Kela, Fimea (medicines), WHO. Almost all link-out.
3. **Safety:** the euthanasia and end-of-life topic needs safe-messaging care (no methods, a help box); abortion and
   fertility treatment need inclusive, non-moralising wording with both legal facts and the ethical positions; the
   disease pages must not read as medical advice and must avoid blaming people for their illness; the vaccine
   section follows the evidence without false balance.
4. **Exam fit:** TE3 suits ethical analysis, research-plan, chart-reading (incidence, inequality) and service-choice
   tasks. A re-mapping of the exam's TE3-touching points should show at least 60% full coverage and none at 0% on the
   first pass, because the topics are written with the mapping in hand.

## The work

1. **Research** (`scout.js`), six researchers:
   - the TE3 curriculum in depth, and how TE3 links to TE1 and TE2 without repeating them;
   - materials to link per TE3 content area, each read for its audience and licence;
   - facts and laws that change (patients' rights, abortion, fertility treatment, the vaccination programme, the
     wellbeing services counties, the costs and fees of services, the medicines rules);
   - disease epidemiology and infectious disease: stable concepts and the official sources for figures;
   - ethics and safety: the ethical-analysis framework, the sensitive questions, safe messaging for end of life;
   - what TE3 must carry for the exam: every TE3-touching task in the brief 003 mapping, with its format.

   The critic proposes the topic list with codes, the link inventory, the safety flags, and which exam rows each
   topic must serve.
2. **Content** (`module-content.js`, module TE3, order 21–30): a data agent (TE3 into `subject.yaml`, codes fetched,
   materials and yearly facts) and the lead writer (TE3 topic 01, style-guide additions) in parallel; three writers;
   four checkers (curriculum, facts and links, safety and inclusion, pedagogy and exam tasks); one fix round; a
   re-mapper of the exam's TE3-touching rows; the judge on the final recheck.
3. The producer runs the gated hygiene scan, commits, pushes and checks the live pages.
